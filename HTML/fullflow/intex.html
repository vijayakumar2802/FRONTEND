DUCATI

The Ducati Panigale V4 is a premium Italian superbike known for its exceptional performance, advanced electronics and aggressive racing-inspired styling. It is designed for high-performance road and track riding.

  • Engine: 998 cc, liquid-cooled, 4-cylinder
  • Power: Around 200+ PS
  • Torque: Around 115 Nm
  • Transmission: 6-speed
  • Fuel Tank: 17 litres
  • Weight: Approximately 207 kg
  • Top Speed: Around 300 km/h
  • Price: Around ₹20.79 lakh (ex-showroom)

YAMAHA R15 v4

The Yamaha R15 V4 is a lightweight, fully-faired sports bike designed for sporty riding and everyday use. It has an aggressive racing-inspired design and is suitable for beginners as well as experienced riders.

  • Engine: 155 cc, liquid-cooled, 4-stroke
  • Power: 18.4 PS
  • Torque: 14.2 Nm
  • Transmission: 6-speed
  • Fuel Tank: 11 litres
  • Weight: Approximately 141 kg
  • Top Speed: Around 140 km/h
  • Price: Around ₹1.71 lakh onwards (ex-showroom)

KAWASAKI NINJA ZX-10R

The Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R is a high-performance litre-class superbike. It features a powerful 998 cc inline-four engine and is heavily inspired by Kawasaki’s racing technology.

  • Engine: 998 cc, liquid-cooled, 4-cylinder
  • Power: Around 200+ PS
  • Torque: Around 115 Nm
  • Transmission: 6-speed
  • Fuel Tank: 17 litres
  • Weight: Approximately 207 kg
  • Top Speed: Around 300 km/h
  • Price: Around ₹20.79 lakh (ex-showroom)


Brands

The motorcycle industry is home to many popular bike brands known for their stylish designs, powerful performance, comfort, and advanced technology. Yamaha is well known for sporty performance and modern design, while Royal Enfield focuses on classic styling, strong road presence, and comfortable long rides. KTM is popular for its aggressive design and high-performance engines, whereas Honda is known for reliability, smooth performance, and everyday practicality.
